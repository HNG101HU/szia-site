FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. All current lessons are free and will stay free. Premium features will be offered separately later.

Does it work offline?

Yes, lessons are bundled with the app. Syncing requires a connection.

My progress doesn't show on another device.

Sign in with the same account on both devices, then Profile → "Sync now". Device and account progress are merged; nothing is deleted.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. Your account and all server data are deleted immediately and permanently. Without access to the app, email us from your registered address.

Is the app available in English?

The interface is currently Turkish only, because the course is designed around Turkish–Hungarian comparisons.
