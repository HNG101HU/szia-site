Hungarian, the Turkish way

Turkish and Hungarian are closer than you think: both agglutinative, both with vowel harmony, neither with grammatical gender — and they share words like alma / elma (apple) and kapu / kapı (gate). Szia turns those similarities into the teaching method. The app interface is in Turkish.
